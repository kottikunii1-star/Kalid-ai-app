# Kalid MS - Android Business Management Application

**Kalid MS** is a professional Android business management application for woodwork and metalwork businesses.

## Features

### 📱 Core Features
- ✅ User Authentication (Register, Login, Multiple Users)
- ✅ Business Profile Management
- ✅ Order Management System
- ✅ Customer Management
- ✅ Automatic Calculations (Area, Pricing)
- ✅ Payment Tracking
- ✅ Comprehensive Reports
- ✅ Dark/Light Mode
- ✅ Multilingual Support (Oromo & English)

### 🎯 Business Management
- Create and manage orders
- Track customer information
- Automatic area and price calculations
- Payment management (Advance & Remaining)
- Order status tracking
- Customer order history

### 📊 Reports
- Total orders report
- Revenue analytics
- Payment summaries
- Daily, Weekly, Monthly reports
- Customer analytics

## Tech Stack

- **Language:** Kotlin/Java
- **IDE:** Android Studio
- **Database:** Room Database (SQLite)
- **Architecture:** MVVM
- **UI Framework:** Material Design 3
- **Languages:** Oromo & English
